How it works

Three big buttons while you drive. A real map when you get home.

The buttons are made to be tapped without looking twice. Better still, hand the phone to a passenger, or pull over first.

  1. 1

    Tap a color and drive

    Tap Green, Yellow or Red. GPS paints the street you're on in that color as you go. No signal? The trip is kept on your phone.

  2. 2

    Switch, pause, note

    Tap a new color when the block changes, Pause to stop painting, and Add note to drop a pin: “vacant, overgrown yard, 412 Maple”.

  3. 3

    Finish and describe the trip

    Name the drive, write what each color meant on this trip, and email yourself a summary. Every trip lands on My map.

  4. 4

    Explore with the numbers

    Every neighborhood on screen is colored by your criteria. Open one for every number behind its score, and save the search to come back to.

The map key

How to read a Green Zone map.

Two kinds of color share the map. Lines are what you painted. Shaded neighborhoods are what the data says, using your criteria.

Streets you painted

  • Green Good: a block you'd buy on.
  • Yellow Mixed: worth a closer look.
  • Red Rough: you'd pass for now.
  • Your colors Pro: any zone you define.
  • 📍Pins Notes you dropped on the drive.

You can rewrite what each color means on the Key page.

Neighborhood scores

  • Green Scores in the top third on your criteria.
  • Yellow In the middle.
  • Red In the bottom third.
  • Dashed outline Misses one of your must-haves.
  • Gray Not enough data to score.

You choose where green starts and red ends.

The data

Free public data, shown with its source.

Every number in Explore says where it came from. Nothing about race, ethnicity, religion, sex, family status or disability is fetched or scored, in line with fair housing.

Zillow ResearchTypical home values by type and bedrooms, asking rents and 12-month change, by ZIP.
Census ACSHome values, rents, income, vacancy, owners vs. renters, age of homes, commuting.
FEMA flood mapsShare of each neighborhood in the 100-year floodplain, plus a flood-zone layer.
FRED (St. Louis Fed)This week's 30-year mortgage rate, for payment and rent-coverage estimates.
CDC PLACESShare of adults who are active in their free time, by tract.
NASA Black MarbleHow bright each area is at night, from satellite imagery.
OpenStreetMapParks, trails, street lighting, transit, groceries, schools and land use.
Census TIGERNeighborhood (tract) and ZIP code boundaries.

Zoning and crime aren't included: there's no free, reliable nationwide source by neighborhood. Check the county zoning map before you buy.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and you're in as a guest, with everything working. Your work stays in that browser until you choose Save my work with email, which signs you in with a 6-digit code (no passwords) and keeps your trips on any device.

Is it safe to use while driving?

The buttons are big and one tap changes the color, but the safest way is to let a passenger tap, or to pull over first. You can also paint later with Draw mode.

What do the colors mean?

Out of the box: green is a block you'd buy on, yellow is mixed, red is one you'd pass on. You can rewrite those meanings on the Key page, and with Pro add your own colors for any purpose.

Where do the neighborhood scores come from?

From free public data (Zillow Research, the Census, FEMA, FRED, CDC, NASA and OpenStreetMap), ranked against the other neighborhoods on screen using the criteria and weights you pick. Your own drive ratings can count too.

Why isn't crime or zoning included?

There's no free, reliable nationwide source at the neighborhood level for either. “Main land use” shows what's mapped on the ground, which isn't the legal zoning. Always check the city or county zoning map.

Who can see my drives?

Only you. Trips, notes, ratings and saved searches belong to your account (or your browser, as a guest) and aren't shared or sold.

Does it work with bad signal?

Yes. The trip in progress is saved on your phone as you go, so a dead zone, a refresh or a closed tab doesn't lose it. It uploads when you finish with signal.
